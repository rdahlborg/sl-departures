SL Departures

Live Stockholm metro departures, and where the next trains are right now, on a small ESP32-C6 display.

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Installing needs a secure (https) page.

Connect the board with a USB-C data cable, click the button, and pick the port. Choose erase for a first install. After flashing, the installer asks for your Wi-Fi name and password, which are sent only to the board. Then Visit device opens the board's page for choosing the line and station. You can also close the Wi-Fi dialog and set up from your phone instead (below).

What you need

  • A Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 (ESP32-C6 with a 1.47" 172×320 display).
  • A USB-C cable that carries data, not just power.
  • Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS.

Using it

The line list has the three lines, then Mobil…, Wi-Fi… and Om / Uppdatera…. Mobil… shows a QR code: scan it with a phone on the same Wi-Fi to choose the line and station there.

The board remembers your station. The map shows four stations each side of yours. Trains are placed from SL's real-time departures at the neighbouring stations and glide along the track, in towards your station and on out the other side. An arrow and a trail mark a train on its way; a blinking outline marks one at the platform.

Wi-Fi setup from your phone

A new board shows a QR code by itself. To change networks later, hold the button, go to Wi-Fi… in the line list, and hold again.

  1. Scan the QR code with your phone camera to join the board's hotspot.
  2. A setup page opens. If it doesn't, scan the second code that appears on the screen.
  3. Pick your network, enter the password, and choose the line and station. The board shows when it's connected.

If the new network doesn't work, the board keeps the old one. From a computer, you can also open this page, connect, and choose Change Wi-Fi.

Updates

The board updates itself over Wi-Fi at night and keeps your Wi-Fi and station. To update right away, open Om / Uppdatera… in the line list and hold the button. If a new version can't fetch departures, the board goes back to the previous one by itself.

Boards installed before v1.1.0 need one update from this page first.

Troubleshooting

  • Empty port list, or Visit device can't reach the board (Mac): allow Chrome under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, then quit and reopen Chrome.
  • Port busy: close other installer tabs, or unplug and replug the board.
  • Dot next to the clock: red means no Wi-Fi, orange means the departure data is more than 90 seconds old.
