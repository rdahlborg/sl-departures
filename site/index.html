SL Departures

Live Stockholm metro departures, and where the next trains are right now, on a small ESP32-C6 display.

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Installing needs a secure (https) page.

Connect the board with a USB-C data cable, click the button, and pick the port. After flashing, the installer asks for your Wi-Fi name and password. They are sent only to the board.

What you need

  • A Waveshare ESP32-C6-LCD-1.47 (ESP32-C6 with a 1.47" 172×320 display).
  • A USB-C cable that carries data, not just power.
  • Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS.

Using it

Everything is done with the BOOT button. RESET just restarts the board.

The board remembers your station. The map shows four stations each side of yours. Trains are placed from SL's real-time departures at the neighbouring stations, so they move along the line as they approach.

Changing Wi-Fi

Open this page again with the board connected, click the button, and choose Change Wi-Fi. Your station is kept.
